Our Story

MSQ is a clothing brand from Kazakhstan founded by Meirzhan Samalbek.

The retail business started in 2015. After rebranding in 2018, it became MSQ.

In a WEproject interview, the founder describes a focus on calm colours, comfortable cuts and basic clothing.

Our website presents the brand, a seasonal collection, size information and store contacts.
